Design notes

Why it looks this way

Intended users

Families, college groups and neighbours looking for a safe, joyful garba night, mostly on phones and sharing the link in group chats.

Style choices

  • Red, orange and gold on warm cream recall diyas and festive clothing.
  • Yatra One headings feel traditional; Poppins keeps text readable.
  • Shared variables control all colours and fonts, with automatic dark mode.

Reusable components

Button, Section header and Card are reused across highlights, schedule and passes.
